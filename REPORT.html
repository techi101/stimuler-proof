Stimuler: what unhappy reviewers say, and four things a growth intern could test

Snapshot 20 Nov 2026. Public store reviews plus the founders' public posts. Review text is what users say, not verified account data.

Why look at this

The founders say retention is P0 and India trails their other big markets: India D30 retention went from 3.8% to 8.7%, against 12.6% in Indonesia and 14.2% in LATAM (Akshay Akash, Sep 2026). His Oct 2026 post: Certain geos like India we were just not able to breakthrough despite multiple attempts (post). Store reviews are a free, daily signal on why people leave.

Four findings

1. Paying users are a third of the angry reviews, and the reply sends them back to the channel that failed. 96 of 275 (34.9%) say they paid; their top issues are bugs (31%), value for money (26%), no support reply (21%) and Pro not active (18%). Of 46 reviews about "paid but no Pro" or "no reply", 39 got a store reply pointing to an email address, while the review says email went unanswered. On iOS, 29 of 64 unhappy reviews say they paid.

2. Some ads set a "free" expectation the paywall breaks. Four App Store India one star reviews name Instagram or social ads, e.g. Downloaded this after getting spammed by really good Instagram ads ... I got the one year Pro subscription for ₹999 (18 Oct) and they have claimed it is completely free. However, once you arrive here, they demand payment (1 Jun).

3. Intermediate learners get bored, while happy users talk about confidence and jobs. 25 complaints say lessons are too basic or repeat. In 992 happy (4 to 5 star) reviews with 5+ words: speaking or fluency 97, confidence 28, interviews, jobs or IELTS 21. Most liked 5 star review in the window (67 thumbs, 5 Jul): my main problem in English language is speaking; most liked one about confidence (18 thumbs, 6 Aug): i feel very confident.

4. Free chatbots are the comparison. ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude are named in 7 unhappy reviews, Duolingo in 8; none in the window named ELSA, Praktika or Loora.

Tool: weekly review alert (alert.py, 13 tests pass)

Tags the last 7 days, compares each theme with the 4 weeks before, flags spikes (3+ reviews and 2x the usual week), and lists payers with money or support problems for a human to reach first. On this snapshot it flagged AI speech complaints in the week to 12 Nov and paywall complaints in the week to 29 Oct.

Limits

  • Newest reviews, not a random sample; unhappy people write more, so shares describe reviewers, not all users.
  • The English Play feed also returns Spanish and Indonesian reviews (34 of the 275), so this is not India only.
  • Themes are keyword rules (English, Hinglish, Spanish, Indonesian). Real rates need Stimuler's payment and support logs. iOS sample is small. Reddit and Trustpilot gave nothing usable.
